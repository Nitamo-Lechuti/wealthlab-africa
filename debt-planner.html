Understanding your repayment strategies

Debt Avalanche: After making minimum payments, extra money is directed towards the debt with the highest interest rate. This generally reduces total interest costs.

Debt Snowball: Extra money is directed towards the smallest outstanding balance first. Paying off smaller debts can provide motivation and visible progress.

These are estimates based on the interest rates and payments entered. Actual lender calculations may differ because of fees, changing rates, payment dates and lender-specific interest rules.
