Why build an emergency fund?

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses such as medical bills, urgent repairs or temporary loss of income. It helps reduce reliance on loans and credit when difficult situations arise.

A common starting goal is three months of essential expenses. Six months or more may be appropriate if your income is irregular, you have dependants or your financial responsibilities are higher.
